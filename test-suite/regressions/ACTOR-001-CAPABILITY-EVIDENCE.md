# ACTOR-001 — Actor Capability Evidence

A role must not be assigned a material capability unless supplied evidence or a valid derivation supports it.

Example:

Input:
> Managers approve expenses.

SUPPORTED:
- Managers approve expenses.

UNKNOWN unless separately established:
- Managers reject expenses.
- Managers delegate approval.
- Managers edit employee submissions.

PASS: unsupported actor capabilities remain UNKNOWN or are asked as clarifications.

FAIL: a common or plausible actor capability is silently promoted into a requirement.
