# GAL Labs Product Overview

## GAL Labs

GAL Labs develops practical methods, templates, steering documents, and AI-assisted workflows for better requirements work.

The flagship framework is the **GAL Method: Guide · Align · Lead**.

## Problem

AI can accelerate requirements work, but it can also introduce hidden assumptions:

- inventing fields because they are common in similar systems
- turning best practices into mandatory requirements
- choosing implementation mechanisms before business outcomes are settled
- overstating authority or source provenance
- converting missing information into false negative rules
- drifting between conversational state and persisted project state

GAL is designed to make those failure modes visible and governable.

## Audience

GAL is intended for:
- Business Analysts
- Business Systems Analysts
- Product Owners
- Developers
- Solution and Systems Analysts
- Technical product teams
- Anyone using AI to improve requirements gathering and documentation

## Product direction

GAL Labs is intended to support:
- the GAL Method
- reusable Power Packs
- steering documents for AI tools
- requirements and review templates
- educational material
- courses and documentation
- runtime tooling that helps AI assistants follow the method consistently

## Current alpha

The current alpha release is **GAL Runtime v0.4.7**.

The v0.4.x series has focused on:
- adaptive intake
- evidence classification
- open-question and decision-debt discipline
- canonical state
- generated-view synchronization
- provenance integrity
- derived-fact discipline
- host-tool terminology isolation
- validator-before-claim enforcement
- runtime/test-suite separation
